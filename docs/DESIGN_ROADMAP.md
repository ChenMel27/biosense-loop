# ExitLoop Research Design Roadmap

## Next content-model improvement: diagnostic map

Replace separate long lists with one teacher-reviewable map:

| Scientific idea | What counts as response evidence | Possible incompatible claim | Follow-up question | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Teacher-defined relationship | Observable wording or reasoning, not keywords alone | Specific explicit claim | One approved probe | Curriculum or research citation |

This is primarily an authoring and content-validity change. It makes the classifier contract easier to inspect because every label, evidence rule, question, and source appears in one row. It should be prototyped with teachers before the stored content-pack schema changes.

## Next dashboard improvement: decision-first review

The dashboard should progressively answer three questions:

1. **What needs attention?** Rank two or three class patterns by prevalence, abstention, and instructional importance without calling them mastery scores.
2. **What supports that signal?** Open a pattern to see exact quotes, full responses, counterexamples, and teacher correction controls.
3. **What will I do next?** Let the teacher choose a brief clarification, response review, temporary group, proceed, or another action, then record confidence and a note.

The current evidence view is the foundation. The next prototype should add filtering, teacher corrections, and a clearer movement from class signal to response evidence to instructional action. Test it through timed teacher tasks and think-aloud interviews before adding more metrics.
