# Draft Cellular-Respiration Matter-and-Energy Rubric

**Status: draft—partner-teacher, biology-reviewer, and pilot validation required before use.**

Use this rubric for the common initial and near-transfer prompts. It is aligned to Georgia GSE S7L4.b: trace matter cycling and energy flow among biotic and abiotic ecosystem components. Do not award or remove points for biochemical stages, ATP yield, organelle detail, or a memorized equation.

## Analytic dimensions

Score each dimension from 0–2, then sum to a 0–8 total. Raters must be blind to condition for the primary near-transfer score.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Matter tracing | Matter is absent, disappears, or becomes energy | Names matter or carbon but traces an incomplete path | Traces food/carbon matter into a scientifically plausible output or ecosystem component and preserves matter as matter |
| Energy flow | Energy is absent or cycles identically to matter | States that energy is used or released without a clear direction | Describes energy transfer/use and recognizes that some energy leaves the ecosystem as heat rather than cycling like matter |
| Cellular-respiration role | Missing, equated only with breathing, or scientifically incompatible | Identifies cellular respiration as related to food, gases, or cells but leaves the relationship incomplete | Explains that cellular respiration in cells releases usable energy from food and appropriately connects it with gas exchange |
| Ecosystem connection | Discusses only an isolated organism | Connects the organism to one living or nonliving component | Connects respiration to matter cycling and energy flow across multiple biotic/abiotic components |

## Alternative-conception codes

Code each as `present`, `absent`, or `insufficient evidence` independently of the rubric score:

- `respiration_is_breathing_only`
- `matter_becomes_energy_or_disappears`
- `plants_do_not_respire`
- `energy_cycles_like_matter`

Do not infer a code from missing vocabulary. Code only an explicit or logically necessary response relationship.

## Scoring rules

1. Score scientific relationships, not grammar, spelling, response length, or exact terminology.
2. Accept everyday language when the relationship is scientifically interpretable.
3. Do not infer an unstated matter or energy path.
4. A contradiction can lower only the dimension it undermines; preserve the other dimension scores.
5. Record `not scorable` separately when an accommodation or technical failure prevents interpretation.
6. Preserve original and adjudicated scores; never overwrite disagreement history.
7. Apply the same rubric and anchors to both conditions and all response stages.

## Required validation work

- Add at least two seventh-grade anchor responses for every dimension level.
- Conduct cognitive interviews or a non-study rehearsal to verify prompt interpretation.
- Have two raters independently score a separate response set and revise ambiguous rules before freezing the rubric.
- Double-code every primary near-transfer response if feasible; approximately 30 responses makes this manageable.
- Report weighted or dimension-level agreement with uncertainty and the adjudication procedure.
- Freeze the rubric version before primary scoring begins.
