# Draft Selective-Permeability Knowledge Integration Rubric

**Status: draft—teacher and biology-reviewer validation required before use.**

Apply this analytic progression to the common initial and near-transfer prompts, then refine the concept-specific descriptors through pilot scoring. Raters must be blind to condition for the primary near-transfer score.

| Score | Knowledge integration descriptor | Selective-permeability evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Blank, off-task, or no scorable scientific idea | No interpretable statement about the membrane or movement |
| 1 | One isolated relevant idea | Names the membrane, crossing, or a needed substance without a relationship |
| 2 | Partial relationship with an important gap or contradiction | States that some substances cross but gives no defensible factor/mechanism or system effect; may also include an incompatible all-or-nothing claim |
| 3 | Connected explanation | Connects selective movement to at least one relevant factor (substance/membrane property, transport protein, or concentration relationship) and an effect on internal cell conditions |
| 4 | Coherent, appropriately bounded explanation | Connects selective boundary, relevant factors/mechanisms, and system effect while avoiding a universal size-only or all-or-nothing claim; qualifies that different substances may use different pathways |

## Scoring rules

1. Score scientific connections, not grammar, spelling, vocabulary count, or response length.
2. Do not infer an unstated mechanism.
3. A response can use different scientifically acceptable wording.
4. If a contradiction undermines the central relationship, the response cannot receive a 4.
5. Record “not scorable” separately when an accommodation or technical failure prevents interpretation.
6. Preserve the original score and adjudicated score; never overwrite disagreement history.

## Required validation work

- Add at least two real or expert-written anchor responses at every attainable score.
- Have two raters independently score a non-study set and discuss descriptor ambiguity.
- Double-code every primary near-transfer response if feasible; with about 30 responses this is manageable.
- Report weighted agreement with uncertainty and the adjudication procedure.
- Freeze this file and its version identifier before primary scoring begins.
