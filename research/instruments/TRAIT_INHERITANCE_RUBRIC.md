# Trait-Inheritance Explanation Rubric

## Construct and use

Use this analytic rubric for the common initial, revised, and near-transfer explanations. It operationalizes Georgia GSE S7L3.a. Do not award or remove points for Punnett squares, probability, dominance terminology, memorized meiosis/mitosis stages, DNA mechanisms, or writing mechanics unless they change the scientific meaning.

Score each dimension 0–2. Total range: 0–8.

| Dimension | 0 — absent/incompatible | 1 — partial | 2 — complete for this task |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gene as inherited trait-related information | Does not connect a gene to the trait, or treats the gene as the visible trait itself | Identifies a gene/gene version related to the trait but leaves information-vs-trait meaning implicit or vague | Explains that a gene/gene version is inherited information related to the trait and distinguishes it from the visible trait |
| Gene–chromosome relationship | Omits chromosomes or states that genes and chromosomes are unrelated | Mentions that the relevant gene and chromosome are connected but does not explain location/carrying role | Explains that the relevant gene/version is located on and carried by the specified chromosome from parent to offspring |
| Contribution from both parents | Omits parental source or attributes the relevant information to only one parent | Mentions both parents or two versions but clearly traces only one contribution | Traces one relevant chromosome/gene version from each parent into the offspring's pair |
| Evidence-linked trait explanation | Gives no evidence-based connection or makes a conclusion incompatible with the card | Uses at least one correct evidence fact or states the correct trait relationship without a complete reasoning link | Uses at least two relevant evidence facts and explains how the inherited version pair supports the observed trait conclusion |

## Evidence-card-specific scoring anchors

Initial beetle task, full-credit evidence can include:

- bristle-shape gene at the marked location on chromosome 3;
- one chromosome 3/gene version inherited from each parent;
- offspring has b and b;
- the card associates b and b with curved bristles.

Near-transfer plant task, full-credit evidence can include:

- seed-coat gene at the marked location on chromosome 2;
- one chromosome 2/gene version inherited from each parent;
- seed has c and c;
- the card associates c and c with spotted seed coats.

Students do not need to use the word “allele” or a slash notation. Accurate age-appropriate equivalents receive credit.

## Additional human codes

Record each as present, absent, or unclear, separately from the 0–8 total:

- `gene_is_the_trait`
- `genes_lack_hereditary_information`
- `genes_and_chromosomes_unrelated`
- `one_parent_determines_trait`
- `parents_contribute_different_traits`
- `acquired_trait_is_inherited`

Do not mark a misconception present solely because a relationship is omitted.

## Revision coding

For initial-to-final analysis, add:

- `target_relationship`: relationship addressed by the displayed prompt;
- `targeted_repair`: 1 if the targeted dimension increases without introducing a new incompatible claim, otherwise 0;
- `revision_type`: conceptual addition, evidence addition, wording-only change, no substantive change, or new incompatibility.

## Blinding and reliability

1. Remove prompt ID, AI tags, confidence, and timestamps from rater files.
2. Train two raters with responses outside the analysis set.
3. Independently double-score at least 20% of responses; for a small pilot, double-score all responses when feasible.
4. Report dimension-level exact/weighted agreement and total-score intraclass correlation or another preselected reliability statistic appropriate to the final data.
5. Resolve disagreements by discussion only after independent scores are saved; retain original and consensus values.
6. Freeze clarifications to the scoring guide before calculating outcomes.
